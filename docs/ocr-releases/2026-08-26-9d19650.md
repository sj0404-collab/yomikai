## Positive qualities

The candidate compares PP-OCRv5 against PP-OCRv3 for every Cyrillic crop and prefers valid Cyrillic PP-OCRv5 output when its script quality supports that decision. In device validation it improved several previously fused captions, including restoring the word boundary and `Ё` in `«ОХОТНИЧИЙ ПЁС»`.

The remote workflow completed the focused OCR test suite, the complete unit-test suite, and the signed APK build. The candidate therefore has reproducible automated validation as well as a real-device feedback record.

## Negative qualities and known limitations

> The build is an improvement, not a finished local OCR solution. New device evidence still shows false word breaks, missed characters, omitted text regions, and no result for some visible Russian text.

The following limitations are confirmed by device feedback:

| Category | Example observed on device | Required follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| False line-break hyphens | `МНЕ ХО- РОШО`, `НЕУПРАВ- ЛЯЕМЫЙ`, `БЕС- ПОЛЕЗНЫЙ` | Join only true line-wrap hyphens before line breaks are normalized. |
| Missed letters or word boundaries | `ДМА` for `ДОМА`; merged or shortened words | Improve candidate choice and retain visual word boundaries without dictionary substitution. |
| Missing result | White boxed Russian caption and outlined dialogue can return `Нет результатов` | Keep whole-line PP-OCRv5 as a candidate and avoid losing it during visual word splitting. |

## Validation evidence

Automated checks passed in the remote workflow. Device screenshots then identified the remaining defects listed above. The next candidate must be tested against those same cases before it is described as a broader repair.
